About the Author

Writer & creator · Light & Love (MW)

I’m a writer and creator who loves pairing soft watercolor art with peaceful, uplifting words. Some of my books are inspired by Bible verses and my Christian faith, while others offer mindful reflections, gentle quotes, and simple joys from everyday life.

Through calming visuals and soul-soothing messages, I hope to create quiet moments of comfort for readers around the world. Whether you’re looking for spiritual encouragement, a thoughtful gift, or a small pocket of peace in your day, my books are here to remind you that life—though imperfect—is still a beautiful gift.

Gift ideas

Birthdays, encouragement parcels, new seasons of marriage, or a soft “thinking of you” — many titles travel well as paper gifts.

Who the books are for

Quiet readers, people of faith, journaling hearts, couples, teens needing a cheer, and anyone craving a calmer page.

Faith + everyday peace

Scripture-rooted devotionals sit beside mindfulness, gratitude, and art books — light for both the prayerful and the weary.

A little light for ordinary days

Choose a book that meets you where you are — faith, gratitude, love, colouring, or quiet encouragement.
